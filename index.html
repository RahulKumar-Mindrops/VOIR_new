Our Promise

See it. Love it. Live it.

Wherever you watch, colour stays honest, sound feels close, and everyday use stays effortless — backed by people who show up long after the unboxing.

Every VOIR is QLED. Quantum-dot colour delivers true-to-life colour, consistent contrast, and exceptional clarity—even in bright rooms. With the Quantum Color Optimizer for scene-tuned colour, Infinity Display for an immersive edgeless view, Pixel Pure Panel for uniform HD-to-4K UHD clarity, and the PQX Pro Elite Suite bringing advanced picture technologies together, VOIR delivers a richer, sharper, more immersive picture.

Cinema-inspired sound and picture work together to create an immersive experience that fills the room while keeping every detail clear and every conversation crisp. Dolby Atmos Vision brings cinematic picture and sound, Sound Spectre Power Boost delivers richer bass and fuller sound, AI Scene Tuning automatically adapts picture and audio to what you’re watching, and VoiceCrisp keeps dialogue clear even when the action gets loud.

Living with VOIR is about making everyday entertainment effortless. SmartTap lets you cast from your phone with a tap, while HomeSync brings your connected devices together. With Google Assistant, simply ask to search, switch inputs, or open an app—and with Service & Parts, you have real people, on-site engineers, and dependable parts support when you need it.
